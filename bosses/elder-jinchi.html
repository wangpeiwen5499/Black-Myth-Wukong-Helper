Strategy打法策略

Phase 1阶段一

Elder Jinchi is a ranged-oriented boss who fires golden Buddhist projectiles in circular patterns. His most common attack is a volley of homing orbs that spread outward in a fan shape. Use the pillars in the arena as cover: projectiles cannot pass through them. Strafe around him at medium range and close the gap only when he finishes a volley. His recovery after a projectile barrage lasts about 2 seconds, which is your window to land a full light-attack combo. Watch for his golden beam attack that sweeps horizontally across the arena — sprint to the far side to avoid it.金池长老是一个远程型Boss，以环形模式发射金色佛珠弹幕。他最常见的攻击是一波扇形散开的追踪弹。利用竞技场中的柱子作为掩体：弹幕无法穿透。在中距离围绕他旋转，只在弹幕齐射结束后拉近距离。弹幕攻击后的收招硬直大约2秒，这是你打出一套完整轻攻击连招的窗口。注意他横扫竞技场的金色光束攻击 — 向远端奔跑来躲避。

Phase 2阶段二

In Phase 2, Elder Jinchi summons golden orbs that home in on your position. These orbs deal moderate damage individually but can stack quickly if ignored. Destroy them with quick light attacks or a heavy attack sweep before refocusing on the boss. After destroying the orbs, he enters a brief recovery state — punish with your heaviest combos. The Immobilize spell (定身术) is extremely effective here: cast it right after he summons orbs to freeze both him and the orbs in place, giving you free time to deal damage.第二阶段，金池长老召唤追踪你的金色光球。这些光球单独伤害不高，但不管的话会迅速叠加。用快速轻攻击或重攻击横扫来摧毁它们，然后再集中攻击Boss。摧毁光球后他会短暂硬直 — 用最重的连招惩罚。定身术在这里极为有效：在他召唤光球后立刻施放，可以同时冻结他和光球，给你充足的输出时间。

Recommended Gear推荐装备
The Immobilize Spell (定身术) is your most valuable tool — Elder Jinchi is particularly weak to it. Pair it with a high-mobility staff that lets you close distance quickly after projectile volleys. Avoid magic-damage weapons since he resists them.定身术是你最有价值的工具 — 金池长老对定身特别弱。搭配高机动性棍棒，让你在弹幕齐射后快速拉近距离。避免使用法术伤害武器，因为他有法术抗性。

Quick Tips实用技巧

  • Use pillars as cover. The arena pillars completely block his projectile attacks. Stay near one and use it to block volleys while closing distance.利用柱子做掩体。竞技场的柱子可以完全挡住弹幕攻击。待在柱子附近，用它来遮挡弹幕的同时拉近距离。
  • Save big spells for Phase 2. The Immobilize spell is wasted in Phase 1 — save it for Phase 2 orb summons where it provides maximum value.把大招留给第二阶段。定身术在第一阶段用处不大 — 留到第二阶段召唤光球时使用，收益最大。
  • Watch the orb summon animation. He raises his staff and glows gold before summoning. This is your cue to get ready to destroy orbs or cast Immobilize.注意光球召唤动画。他在召唤前会举起法杖并发出金光。这是你准备摧毁光球或施放定身术的信号。
  • Don't chase him. He moves around the arena during Phase 1. Let him come to you or use pillars to funnel his movement.不要追着他跑。他在第一阶段会在竞技场中移动。让他主动靠近你，或利用柱子限制他的走位。
  • Reward: Fireproof Mantle (避火罩). Defeating him grants the Fireproof Mantle, which is essential for the Black Bear Spirit fight. Do not skip this boss.奖励：避火罩。击败他可获得避火罩，这对黑熊精战至关重要。不要跳过这个Boss。

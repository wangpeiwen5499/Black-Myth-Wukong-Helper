Strategy / 打法策略打法策略

Phase 1 / 阶段一阶段一

The Black Bear Spirit uses heavy slam attacks with wide hitboxes. His primary combo is a 3-hit claw sequence: right swipe, left swipe, overhead slam. Dodge roll through the side swipes and wait for the overhead slam to land before punishing. The slam has a long recovery animation where he pulls his arm from the ground — this is your main damage window. He also has a forward fire breath attack in a cone shape. When you see him rear back, sprint to his flank; the fire breath locks him in place for several seconds. Avoid staying directly in front of him at all times.黑熊精使用范围很大的重砸攻击。他的主要连招是三连爪击：右扫、左扫、过头砸地。翻滚穿过侧扫，等过头砸地落地后再反击。砸地后他拔出手臂的动画很长 — 这是你的主要输出窗口。他还有一个前方锥形火焰吐息。看到他后仰时就向侧翼跑；火焰吐息会让他原地锁定数秒。任何时候都不要待在他正前方。

Phase 2 / 阶段二 (HP < 50%)阶段二 (HP < 50%)

At 50% HP, the Black Bear Spirit transforms and gains devastating charge attacks. His claw combos now leave fire trails on the ground that persist for several seconds, restricting your movement. He gains a new charging bear rush that covers the full length of the arena — dodge perpendicular to the charge direction. The most dangerous new ability is an area-of-effect fire burst: he slams both hands down and sends a shockwave of flame in all directions. When you see him raise both arms high, immediately create maximum distance. Activate the Fireproof Mantle (避火罩) during this transition to negate the burning ground damage. Focus on hit-and-run: land 2-3 attacks after dodging his combos, then back off. Patience is the key to this phase.血量降到50%时，黑熊精变身并获得毁灭性的冲锋攻击。爪击连招现在会在地面留下持续数秒的火焰轨迹，限制你的移动。新增一招覆盖整个竞技场长度的熊冲 — 垂直于冲锋方向闪避。最危险的新技能是全屏火焰爆发：他双手高举猛砸地面，向四面八方释放火焰冲击波。看到他双臂高举时，立刻拉开最大距离。在转换阶段激活避火罩来抵消地面燃烧伤害。采用游击战术：闪避连招后打2-3下就撤退。耐心是这一阶段的关键。

Recommended Gear推荐装备
Equip the Fireproof Mantle (避火罩), rewarded from defeating Elder Jinchi, before entering this fight. It is absolutely essential for Phase 2. Pair it with a Fire-enhanced weapon to exploit his fire weakness. A high-dodge build with extended i-frames will help survive his fast Phase 2 combos.进入这场战斗前装备从金池长老处获得的避火罩。它在第二阶段绝对不可或缺。搭配火属性强化武器来针对他的火焰弱点。延长无敌帧的高闪避构筑有助于存活他的快速二阶段连招。

Quick Tips / 实用技巧实用技巧

  • Activate Fireproof Mantle at Phase 2 transition. The transformation animation gives you enough time to cast it. This negates all fire trail damage during his most dangerous phase.在第二阶段转换时激活避火罩。变身动画给你足够的时间施放。这能抵消他最危险阶段的所有火焰轨迹伤害。
  • Stay close to avoid the charge. His charge attack only triggers when you're at medium-to-long range. Staying in melee range limits him to his claw combos, which are easier to dodge.保持近身来避免冲锋。冲锋攻击只在中远距离触发。待在近战距离限制他使用爪击连招，更容易闪避。
  • Heal during recovery windows. After his 3-hit combo or fire breath, he has a long recovery. Use this time to heal if needed rather than attacking greedily.在收招空隙治疗。三连击或火焰吐息后，他有很长的收招硬直。需要时优先治疗，不要贪刀。
  • Don't lock on during charge attacks. The charge covers too much distance for lock-on camera to track smoothly. Unlock the camera and dodge perpendicular to the charge direction.冲锋攻击时不要锁定。冲锋距离太远，锁定镜头跟不上。解除锁定，垂直于冲锋方向闪避。
  • This is the Chapter 1 finale. Defeating the Black Bear Spirit triggers the end-of-chapter cinematic and unlocks Chapter 2: Yellow Wind Ridge. Make sure you've explored everything before entering Black Wind Cave.这是第一章的压轴战。击败黑熊精会触发章节结束CG并解锁第二章：黄风岭。进入黑风洞前确保已探索完所有内容。

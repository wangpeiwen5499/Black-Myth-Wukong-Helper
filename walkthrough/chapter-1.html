Chapter 1: Fire and Black Clouds第一章：火照黑云

Black Wind Mountain — Where the journey begins.黑风山 — 旅程由此开始。

Chapter Overview章节概览

Map coming soon地图即将推出

Chapter 1 takes place on Black Wind Mountain (黑风山). It serves as the game's introductory area, teaching core mechanics such as combat, dodging, spell casting, and Spirit absorption. The chapter features three main bosses and a handful of optional encounters. Completing it unlocks Chapter 2: Yellow Wind Ridge.第一章发生在黑风山。这是游戏的入门区域，教授核心机制，包括战斗、闪避、法术施放和精魄吸收。本章包含三个主要Boss和若干可选遭遇战。完成后将解锁第二章：黄风岭。

Key Items关键物品

  • Fireproof Mantle (避火罩) — Reduces fire damage; essential for the Black Bear Spirit fight避火罩 — 减少火焰伤害；对抗黑熊精的关键道具

Area 1: Front Hollows区域 1：前山空地

After the opening cinematic, you awaken at the foot of Black Wind Mountain. The first area is a relatively open grove with a few scattered enemies. This section functions as a tutorial, gradually introducing light attacks, heavy attacks, dodging, and the Gourd healing flask. Take your time to practice the timing of your dodges here — the i-frames (invincibility frames) in Black Myth: Wukong are generous, but mastering them early will pay off enormously in later chapters.开场CG结束后，你将在黑风山脚下醒来。第一个区域是一片相对开阔的树林，散布着少量敌人。这一段相当于教学关卡，逐步介绍轻攻击、重攻击、闪避和葫芦治疗。请在这里多花时间练习闪避时机 — 黑神话：悟空的无敌帧比较宽裕，但尽早掌握将在后续章节带来巨大收益。

Before You Start出发前
Make sure to explore the starting area thoroughly. You'll find Gourd upgrades and Spirit absorb items. Breakable pots and crates often contain Willow (柳木) and other crafting materials. Don't rush past them.确保彻底探索起始区域，你会发现葫芦升级和精魄吸收物品。可破坏的坛罐中常含有柳木等制作材料，不要匆匆走过。

Follow the path forward and you'll encounter your first group of basic enemies — skeletal warriors and wolf-like creatures. These enemies telegraph their attacks clearly, so use them to learn the parry window. After clearing the initial grove, you'll reach the first Shrine (土地庙). Shrines act as checkpoints: resting at one refills your Gourd charges, saves your progress, and allows you to allocate skill points. The first Shrine is called Front Hollows Shrine.沿着前方路径前进，你将遇到第一批基础敌人 — 骷髅兵和狼形生物。这些敌人的攻击前摇很明显，可以利用它们来学习弹反窗口。清理完初始林地后，你将到达第一个土地庙。土地庙相当于存档点：休息会补满葫芦次数、保存进度，并允许你分配技能点。第一个土地庙名为前山空地土地庙。

Just past the Shrine, look to the left side of the path. A narrow trail leads uphill to a clearing where the Wandering Wight (游僧) can be found sitting calmly. This is an optional boss and is significantly harder than anything else in the area at this stage. If you're struggling, you can skip it and return later with upgraded stats and better equipment. Defeating it rewards the Wandering Wight Spirit, which is one of the strongest early-game Spirit abilities — a ground slam that deals massive stagger damage.过了土地庙之后，注意路径左侧。一条窄道通向山坡上的一片空地，游僧就安静地坐在那里。这是一个可选Boss，难度明显高于当前区域的其他敌人。如果打不过，可以先跳过，等升级装备后再回来。击败它可获得游僧精魄，这是游戏前期最强的精魄技能之一 — 一次地面重击，造成大量削韧伤害。

From the Shrine, the main path continues deeper into the mountain through a rocky canyon. You'll encounter a few more enemy ambushes along the way — particularly watch for enemies hiding behind rock formations. Keep your guard up and progress toward the Forest of Wolves. Before leaving, make sure you've activated the Shrine so you can fast-travel back if needed.从土地庙出发，主路穿过一段岩石峡谷继续深入山区。沿途会遇到更多伏击敌人 — 特别要留意躲在岩石后面的。保持警惕，朝狼之森林方向前进。离开前确保已激活土地庙，这样需要时可以快速传送回来。

Area 2: Forest of Wolves区域 2：狼之森林

The Forest of Wolves is a densely wooded area filled with wolf-type enemies. These foes are faster than the skeletons from the previous area and tend to attack in packs. Their most dangerous attack is a leaping lunge that covers significant distance — dodge sideways (not backward) to avoid it cleanly. You'll also encounter larger wolf enemies wearing armor; these have a sweeping bite combo that can catch you off-guard if you're too aggressive.狼之森林是一片茂密的林区，遍布狼型敌人。这些敌人比前一区域的骷髅兵更快，而且倾向于成群攻击。它们最危险的攻击是一记远距离跳跃扑击 — 向侧面闪避（而非后退）可以干净利落地躲避。你还会遇到体型更大的装甲狼兵；它们有一套横扫撕咬连招，如果你攻击过于激进，很容易被打个措手不及。

As you push through the forest, keep an eye out for a small side path on the right that leads to a hidden clearing. Here you'll find a Gourd upgrade item sitting on a stone altar. This upgrade increases your healing charges by one, which makes a significant difference in the upcoming boss fights. There's also a crafting material node near the edge of the clearing — collect it for later weapon upgrades.在穿越森林时，注意右侧一条通往隐蔽空地的小径。那里有一个石台，上面放着葫芦升级物品。这次升级会增加一次治疗次数，对接下来的Boss战影响很大。空地边缘还有一个采集点 — 收集它留作后续武器升级之用。

Wandering Wight Boss Fight游僧 Boss 战
If you decided to fight the Wandering Wight earlier, here's the strategy: stay close and circle to his left (your right). His grab attack has a long wind-up but tracks aggressively — dodge at the last moment. Use the Immobilize spell (定身术) to freeze him, then land a full heavy attack combo. Spirit abilities that deal stagger damage are extremely effective against him. Don't get greedy — two to three hits, then reposition.如果你决定挑战游僧，策略如下：贴近他并绕到他的左侧（你的右侧）。他的抓取攻击前摇很长但追踪性极强 — 在最后一刻闪避。使用定身术冻结他，然后打出一套完整的重攻击连招。造成削韧伤害的精魄技能对他非常有效。不要贪刀 — 打两三下就重新站位。

Continue through the forest until you reach the second Shrine, Forest of Wolves Shrine. This is a good place to spend any accumulated skill points. Consider investing in the dodge-extending skill tree and the increased Gourd healing potency. From this Shrine, the path forks: the main route leads forward toward the Snake Path, while a dead-end trail to the left contains a miniboss encounter with a heavily armored wolf commander. Defeating him drops a rare crafting material.继续穿越森林直到到达第二个土地庙 — 狼之森林土地庙。这里适合花掉积累的技能点。建议优先投入闪避延伸技能树和葫芦治疗增效。从这个土地庙开始，路径分岔：主路通向前方的蛇道，左侧的死路有一场小型Boss战，对手是一只重甲狼统领。击败它会掉落稀有制作材料。

Area 3: Snake Path区域 3：蛇道

The Snake Path is a winding mountain trail that lives up to its name. Narrow ledges, falling rocks, and snake-type enemies make this section treacherous. The snake enemies here have a poison spit attack that leaves a damaging area on the ground — avoid standing in the green clouds. Their melee attacks are relatively slow, so punish their whiffs with a quick light-attack combo followed by a dodge-cancel.蛇道是一条名副其实的蜿蜒山路。狭窄的岩架、落石和蛇型敌人让这一段变得危险。这里的蛇兵有喷毒攻击，会在地面留下持续伤害区域 — 避免站在绿色毒雾中。它们的近战攻击相对较慢，所以可以抓住它们攻击落空的间隙，用轻攻击连招反击后闪避取消。

About halfway through the Snake Path, you'll find the Snake Path Shrine. Rest here and prepare for a miniboss encounter just ahead — a giant snake coiled around a ruined pagoda. This miniboss has two key attacks: a wide tail sweep and a forward lunging bite. The tail sweep can be dodged by jumping, while the lunge requires a well-timed sideways dodge. The arena has limited space, so be mindful of your positioning near the edges. Defeating this miniboss rewards a Vessel (法宝) slot upgrade and opens the path to the Ancient Temple.蛇道中段可以找到蛇道土地庙。在这里休息，为前方的迷你Boss战做好准备 — 一条盘绕在废墟宝塔上的巨蛇。这个迷你Boss有两个关键攻击：大范围扫尾和前扑撕咬。扫尾可以跳跃躲避，而前扑需要精确的侧向闪避。战斗场地空间有限，要注意边缘站位。击败它可获得法宝槽位升级，并打开通往古庙的道路。

Near the end of the Snake Path, check the cliffs on the right side for the Immortal Tiger Blade (虎筋刀) material. It's easy to miss because the path to it is partially hidden behind a waterfall. This material is used to craft one of the best early-game weapons, so it's absolutely worth the detour. Once you've collected it, continue forward to the Ancient Temple area.在蛇道末端，检查右侧悬崖，可以找到虎筋刀材料。很容易错过，因为通往它的路径部分隐藏在瀑布后面。这个材料用于打造游戏前期最好的武器之一，绝对值得绕路去取。收集后，继续前进前往古庙区域。

Area 4: Ancient Temple & Black Wind Cave区域 4：古庙与黑风洞

The Ancient Temple is a hauntingly beautiful area filled with incense smoke and crumbling statues. Approach the main hall to trigger the Elder Jinchi (金池长老) boss fight. Elder Jinchi is a ranged-oriented boss who summons golden Buddhist projectiles in circular patterns. The key to this fight is constant movement — strafe around him at medium range and close the gap only when he finishes a projectile volley. His most dangerous move is a golden beam attack that sweeps across the arena; sprint to the sides to avoid it. Use the Immobilize spell to pin him down and unleash your heaviest combos. Defeating Elder Jinchi rewards you with the Fireproof Mantle (避火罩), which is critical for the upcoming final boss.古庙是一个凄美而阴森的区域，弥漫着香烟和残破的佛像。走近正殿会触发金池长老Boss战。金池长老是一个远程型Boss，会以环形模式发射金色佛珠弹幕。这场战斗的关键是持续移动 — 在中距离围绕他旋转，只在弹幕齐射结束后拉近距离。他最危险的招式是一道横扫竞技场的金色光束；向两侧奔跑来躲避。使用定身术钉住他，然后释放最重的连招。击败金池长老可获得避火罩，这对即将到来的最终Boss至关重要。

Preparing for the Black Bear Spirit黑熊精战前准备
Equip the Fireproof Mantle before entering Black Wind Cave. The Black Bear Spirit deals heavy fire damage, and without this item, many of his attacks will one-shot you. Also stock up on Gourd upgrades and make sure your skill points are allocated. Visit the Shrine just outside the cave entrance as your last checkpoint.进入黑风洞前装备避火罩。黑熊精造成大量火焰伤害，没有这件道具的话，他的很多攻击都能一击必杀。同时确保葫芦已升级，技能点已分配。洞穴入口外的土地庙是你的最后一个存档点。

After defeating Elder Jinchi, the path behind the temple altar opens to reveal the entrance to Black Wind Cave (黑风洞). The cave interior is dark and filled with fire-type enemies. Use the Fireproof Mantle's active ability to create a temporary fire shield as you push through. The cave itself is relatively linear with only one major branching path (which leads to a minor loot chest). Follow the burning braziers to stay on the main route.击败金池长老后，寺庙祭坛后面的通道打开，露出黑风洞的入口。洞穴内部阴暗，充斥着火焰型敌人。在推进过程中使用避火罩的主动技能来创建临时火焰护盾。洞穴本身比较线性，只有一条主要岔路（通向一个小型宝箱）。沿着燃烧的火盆前进即可保持正确路线。

The Black Bear Spirit (黑熊精) awaits at the deepest chamber of the cave. This is the final boss of Chapter 1 and has two phases. In Phase 1, he uses sweeping claw combos and fire breath in a forward cone. Dodge roll through the claw swings and flank him from the side during fire breath. At 50% health, he enters Phase 2: he grows larger, his attacks gain fire trails, and he gains a devastating slam attack that sends shockwaves across the arena. The Fireproof Mantle is essential here — activate it during his Phase 2 fire trails to negate the burning ground damage. Focus on hit-and-run tactics: land two to three attacks after dodging his combos, then back off. Patience is the key to this fight. Upon defeating the Black Bear Spirit, you'll receive his Spirit and trigger the end-of-chapter cinematic, unlocking Chapter 2.黑熊精守在洞穴最深处。这是第一章的最终Boss，拥有两个阶段。第一阶段，他使用横扫爪击连招和前方锥形火焰吐息。翻滚穿过爪击，在火焰吐息时从侧面迂回。血量降到50%时进入第二阶段：体型增大，攻击附带火焰轨迹，并新增一招全屏冲击波的砸地攻击。避火罩在这里不可或缺 — 在他第二阶段的火焰轨迹期间激活，抵消地面燃烧伤害。采用游击战术：闪避他的连招后打两三下就撤退。耐心是这场战斗的关键。击败黑熊精后，你将获得他的精魄并触发章节结束CG，解锁第二章。
